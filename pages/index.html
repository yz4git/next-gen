WHY WORLDSEED

Not just a city viewer.

WorldSeed is designed as an open pipeline that other games, simulations, tools, and engines can consume.

01

Real open data

Combine Overture Maps, OpenStreetMap, terrain data, and optional local PLATEAU CityGML with provenance and attribution intact.

02

Gameplay structure

Generate terrain-following roads, dedicated junction geometry, colliders, road graphs, routes, and spawn points instead of geometry alone.

03

Reusable exports

Export GLB plus schema-versioned JSON so downstream tools can consume the world without importing WorldSeed runtime code.
