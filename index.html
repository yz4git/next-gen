LOCATION PRIVACY

Before using your location

After browser permission, WorldSeed rounds your position to six decimals and immediately grows a live world around it.

  • The exact center and radius are sent to public OpenStreetMap Overpass services.
  • Overture tile requests, hosted on Amazon S3, reveal the selected map area.
  • Open terrain tile requests, also hosted on Amazon S3, reveal the selected map area.
  • Results can be cached in this browser for up to 7 days.
  • The app adds no account, analytics, or WorldSeed location server.

Providers and the site host can still receive normal network metadata such as your IP address.

v0.1.1 PUBLIC SAFETY

Privacy & data

Checking this tab’s URL…

Live requests

Overpass receives the selected center and radius. Overture and Mapzen terrain tile requests on Amazon S3 identify the selected area. Those providers and the site host may process IP addresses and other standard request metadata.

On this device

World responses are cached in IndexedDB for up to 7 days. The offline app shell is stored separately without coordinate query strings. Selected PLATEAU CityGML files are parsed in memory, are not uploaded, and are not added to that cache.

WorldSeed itself

This client app adds no user account, analytics tracker, advertising SDK, or WorldSeed backend database. Browser and hosting behavior is governed by their own policies.

Sharing & exports

Coordinates enter a URL only when you choose “exact seed link.” Exports omit the origin by default, but recognizable geometry can still reveal a place.
